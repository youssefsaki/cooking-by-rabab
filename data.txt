A Rare Marketing Framework for Taghazout Cooking Class
Prepared for: Rabab's Kitchen / Taghazout Cooking Class
Website: https://www.taghazout-cooking-class.com
Date: July 2026

Executive Summary
Most cooking classes in Taghazout compete the same way: Instagram photos, Google Ads, TripAdvisor, and hope tourists find them.

This strategy does the opposite. It does not fight for tourist attention in a crowded feed. Instead, it owns three invisible channels almost nobody in Morocco's experience market is using systematically:

Distribution hijacking — become the default "non-surf day" through surf camps and riads, not ads
Weather-triggered demand capture — sell automatically when surf is flat
Memory infrastructure — turn every guest into a permanent, shareable, AI-indexable marketing asset
Combined with citation moat engineering (dominating AI search) and a WhatsApp-first conversion system, this creates a compounding growth loop that gets stronger every week — without increasing ad spend linearly.

Expected outcome (90 days): 40–60% of bookings from owned/partner channels vs. paid ads; top-3 AI citation for "things to do in Taghazout besides surfing."

"Don't find tourists. Be where tourists already are."

Instead of paying Meta to reach strangers, become the official recommended experience inside the places that already hold your customer.

Taghazout has 30+ surf camps. Every camp guest asks: "What do we do on a flat day?"

Free staff experience (1x/month)
Link in their "Things to Do" page
"Flat Day Special" code for their guests
Thursday event cross-promotion

"Your guests always ask what to do when there's no waves. We handle it — pickup included, €60, half-day in the Atlas Mountains. You earn €9 per guest, zero work. We give your staff a free class every month."

Pillar 2: The Rain Day Protocol
"Sell when the ocean says no."

Taghazout tourism is surf-driven. When swell is flat, rain comes, or wind is onshore, thousands of tourists have nothing planned — and nobody systematically captures that moment.

Weather-triggered marketing system
Data source: Windguru, Magicseaweed, or Surfline API for Taghazout Bay

Trigger rules:

Condition	Action
Swell < 0.8m for 2+ days
Activate "Flat Day" campaign
Rain forecast in Taghazout
Push "Rain Day Cooking" message
Thursday + good weather
Push Weekly Amazigh Event
Last day of week for tourist (Fri/Sat checkout)
"Last chance" urgency message
Channel activation when triggered:

Google Ads — bid +40% on "things to do taghazout" keywords only on flat days
Meta geo-fence — show Reels to people currently in Taghazout/Tamraght radius
WhatsApp broadcast to partner surf camps: "Flat tomorrow — send your guests our way, 15% commission"
Instagram Story from Rabab: voice note in French/German "The ocean is resting today... come cook with me"
Brand positioning:

Own the phrase: "The Flat Day Company"

Not "a cooking class" — the default answer when surf doesn't happen. This is identity marketing, not feature marketing.

Why this is rare: Weather-triggered campaigns exist for e-commerce (umbrellas when it rains). Almost zero experience operators in surf towns use swell data as a demand signal. You would be first in Taghazout.

Pillar 3: Memory Infrastructure
"The experience doesn't end when the guest leaves. It starts marketing."

Most operators: guest comes → guest posts Instagram → guest leaves → marketing stops.

Memory Infrastructure: every guest gets a permanent, personalized, shareable page that markets for you forever.

The Recipe Ledger System
48 hours after each class, guest receives WhatsApp message:

"Salam [Name]! Rabab saved your recipes from yesterday. Your personal page is ready:
taghazout-cooking-class.com/memory/sarah-2026-04-12
Share it with friends who ask 'what did you do in Morocco?' 🇲🇦"

Each memory page contains:

3–5 photos from their class (with permission)
Rabab's recipes they learned (tagine, bread, amlou)
Their quote/testimonial
"Book the same experience" CTA
Structured data (Review schema) for AI indexing
Why this is powerful:

Effect	Mechanism
Word of mouth
Guest shares link with family/friends planning Morocco trips
SEO
Hundreds of indexable pages with "Taghazout cooking class" keywords
AI citations
AI finds real guest stories with dates, names, specifics
Social proof
Authentic UGC without paying influencers
Re-booking
Guest returns next year, brings friends
Math: 4 guests/day × 250 days = 1,000 memory pages/year. Each page is a permanent marketing node. After 2 years, you have 2,000 indexed pages competitors cannot replicate.

Why this is rare: Airbnb does photo albums. Almost no day-experience operator builds indexable, structured, permanent guest pages. This is your UGC moat.

Pillar 4: Citation Moat (AI Entity Dominance)
"Don't rank on Google. Become the answer AI gives."

By 2026, 30–40% of travel research starts in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews — not traditional search. Most businesses optimize their website. Almost nobody engineers the entire citation ecosystem AI reads.

The goal
When someone asks any AI:

"What are the best things to do in Taghazout besides surfing?"
"Where can I book a cooking class near Taghazout?"
"How much does a Moroccan cooking class cost in Taghazout?"

The answer must be: Rabab's Taghazout Cooking Class — with price, pickup, and booking link.

Pillar 5: WhatsApp Concierge Funnel
"The website is the brochure. WhatsApp is the salesperson."

In Morocco tourism, WhatsApp converts 3–5x better than web forms. Most Western-marketed experiences treat WhatsApp as a sidebar button. This strategy makes it the primary conversion engine.

The funnel architecture
AWARENESS (Ads, partners, AI, memory pages)
    ↓
INTEREST → Lands on website OR clicks WhatsApp directly
    ↓
CONSIDERATION → WhatsApp conversation (not form)
    ↓
BOOKING → Confirmed via WhatsApp + calendar
    ↓
EXPERIENCE → Class day
    ↓
MEMORY → Recipe page + review request
    ↓
REFERRAL → Guest shares memory page / refers friend
    ↓
(loop back to AWARENESS)
WhatsApp automation sequence
Message 1 — Instant reply (auto):

"Salam! Thanks for reaching out about Rabab's cooking class 🍲
Quick info: Basic €60 · Private €100 · Pickup from Taghazout included.
When are you in Taghazout? I'll check availability."

Message 2 — After they give dates (manual or semi-auto):

Voice note from Rabab (30 sec, French or German if detected):
"Salam, I'm Rabab. I'd love to cook with you in my village..."

Message 3 — Booking confirmation:

Calendar link + what to bring + pickup time + Google Maps pin

Message 4 — Day before:

"See you tomorrow at 9am at Taghazout Mosque! Wear comfortable shoes — we're going to the mountains 🏔️"

Message 5 — 2 hours after class:

"Hope you loved today! Your recipe page: [memory URL]. Would mean the world if you leave a Google review: [link]"

Message 6 — 7 days later (if no review):

"Quick favour — 30 seconds to leave a review helps other travellers find us: [link]"

Why voice notes from Rabab are marketing gold
Personal — tourists feel they know her before arriving
Trust — voice builds connection text cannot
Differentiation — no competitor sends host voice notes
Multilingual — French for French tourists, German for German (your site already supports both)
Shareable — guests forward voice notes to travel partners
Rare tactic: Record 5 Rabab voice notes (EN/FR/DE) for common questions. Use as WhatsApp quick replies AND Meta ad audio.
